A shopping assistant on your store answers customers' questions about products, prices and stock. This guide builds one with Webtalkbot, which reads your catalogue through MCP for WooCommerce. {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Prerequisites {{ anchor: 'prerequisites' }}
You need three things first:

1. **Install MCP for WooCommerce Plugin**: Download and install the plugin from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/iOSDevSK/mcp-for-woocommerce/releases)
2. **Generate JWT Token**: Create a JWT token in your WordPress admin (**Settings > MCP for WooCommerce**)
3. **Webtalkbot Account**: Sign up at <a href="https://webtalkbot.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">webtalkbot.com</a>

## Step 1: Choose WooCommerce Data Source {{ anchor: 'choose-data-source' }}
In your Webtalkbot dashboard:
1. Navigate to "Create New Chatbot"
2. Select **"WooCommerce"** as your data source
3. This connects directly to your store and extracts product data automatically

## Step 2: Configure Your AI Agent {{ anchor: 'configure-ai-agent' }}
Fill in the required configuration:

**Chatbot Name**
- Pick a name customers will see, for example "Shop Assistant"

**Website URL** 
- Your store's full address, including `https://` (for example `https://your-shop.com`)

**Woo-MCP JWT Token**
- The JWT token from **Settings > MCP for WooCommerce**, Authentication Tokens tab
- The field accepts up to 250 characters

## Step 3: Deploy Your Shopping Assistant {{ anchor: 'deploy-assistant' }}

Once it is created, the assistant can:

**Help customers find products**
- Search the catalogue with the customer's own words
- Suggest products that match what they describe
- Give current prices and stock, read live from the store

**Answer support questions**
- Shipping, returns and other policies, from your pages
- Details of a specific product
- Where to find something in the shop

## Step 4: Integration Options {{ anchor: 'integration-options' }}
Deploy your AI assistant using multiple methods:

**Chat bubble**
- A chat window floating on your store pages, for example on product pages and at checkout

**Direct link**
- The assistant on a page of its own, which you can link from emails or campaigns

## Best Practices {{ anchor: 'best-practices' }}
- Keep product descriptions current; the assistant answers from them
- Ask it the questions your customers ask most, and check the answers
- Put the chat bubble on the pages with the most visitors
- Every conversation uses Webtalkbot credits, so keep an eye on your balance
