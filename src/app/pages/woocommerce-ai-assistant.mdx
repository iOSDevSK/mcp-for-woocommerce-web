Transform your WooCommerce store with an intelligent shopping assistant that helps customers find products and increases sales. Here's how to create one using Webtalkbot: {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Prerequisites {{ anchor: 'prerequisites' }}
Before creating your AI assistant, ensure you have:

1. **Install MCP for WooCommerce Plugin**: Download and install the plugin from [GitHub releases](https://github.com/iOSDevSK/mcp-for-woocommerce/releases)
2. **Generate JWT Token**: Create a JWT token in your WordPress admin (**Settings > MCP for WooCommerce**)
3. **Webtalkbot Account**: Sign up at <a href="https://webtalkbot.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">webtalkbot.com</a>

## Step 1: Choose WooCommerce Data Source {{ anchor: 'choose-data-source' }}
In your Webtalkbot dashboard:
1. Navigate to "Create New Chatbot"
2. Select **"WooCommerce"** as your data source
3. This connects directly to your store and extracts product data automatically

## Step 2: Configure Your AI Agent {{ anchor: 'configure-ai-agent' }}
Fill in the required configuration:

**Chatbot Name**
- Choose a descriptive name (e.g., "Shop Assistant", "Product Helper")
- This will be displayed to your customers

**Website URL** 
- Enter your complete WooCommerce store URL
- Include the full URL with `https://` (e.g., `https://your-shop.com`)

**Woo-MCP JWT Token**
- Paste the JWT token from your WordPress admin
- Found in **Settings > MCP for WooCommerce** after plugin installation
- Maximum 250 characters

## Step 3: Deploy Your Shopping Assistant {{ anchor: 'deploy-assistant' }}

Once created, your AI agent provides:

**Smart Product Discovery**
- Natural language product searches
- Intelligent recommendations based on customer needs
- Real-time inventory and pricing information

**Customer Support**
- Answers questions about policies, shipping, returns
- Provides detailed product information
- Guides customers through the shopping process

## Step 4: Integration Options {{ anchor: 'integration-options' }}
Deploy your AI assistant using multiple methods:

**Chat Bubble Widget**
- Floating chat interface on your store pages
- Perfect for product pages and checkout

**Direct Link**
- Standalone assistant for customer support
- Can be used in marketing campaigns

## Best Practices {{ anchor: 'best-practices' }}
- **Keep product data updated**: The AI learns from your product descriptions
- **Test regularly**: Try common customer questions to optimize performance
- **Strategic placement**: Add chat widgets to high-traffic pages
- **Monitor usage**: Each interaction uses credits, plan accordingly

Your WooCommerce AI assistant will help reduce cart abandonment, increase customer satisfaction, and boost sales through intelligent product recommendations.
